Privacy Policy

App: Haber Küpü (the "App") Developer: The Architect Effective Contact: byarchitect@disroot.org

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.
1. Summary

    The App does not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.
    Your in-app settings and preferences are stored only on your device.
    The App shows advertisements through Google AdMob, which may collect and use certain device information (such as your advertising ID) as described in Section 4.
    We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information the App handles
2.1 Information stored on your device

The App saves your preferences and settings (for example, the options or categories you choose) in your device's local storage. This data stays on your device, is not sent to us, and is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.
2.2 Information exchanged when loading content

To display content, the App makes network requests to haberkupu.com.tr. Like any internet request, these requests necessarily transmit technical information such as your IP address, device type, operating system version, and app version to the server that receives them. We do not use this information to identify you personally or to build user profiles.
2.3 Network status

The App checks whether your device is connected to the internet (for example, Wi-Fi or mobile data) so it can load content or tell you when you are offline. This check happens on your device and the result is not collected by us.
2.4 App version information

The App reads its own version number locally, for example to display it in the interface or help with support requests.
2.5 Information you send us

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message. We use it only to respond to you.
3. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

    operate the App and deliver its content;
    remember your preferences;
    display advertising that supports the App;
    diagnose problems and keep the App secure and working; and
    respond to your questions and requests.

4. Advertising and third-party services

The App uses Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC, to display ads. AdMob and its partners may collect and process:

    your device's advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS);
    your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
    device and app information (device model, OS version, app name and version, language);
    ad interaction data (such as ad views and clicks); and
    diagnostic and performance data.

Google uses this information to serve and measure ads, to personalize ads where permitted, and to prevent fraud and abuse. We do not receive your personal profile from Google. For details, see:

    Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
    How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
    AdMob and Firebase data disclosures: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

5. Links to other websites

The App may open external links (for example, to a source website) in your browser or another app. These sites are operated by third parties, and we are not responsible for their content or privacy practices. Please review their privacy policies.
6. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Information may be shared only:

    with service providers acting on our behalf, such as Google AdMob (Section 4);
    when required by law, court order, or a valid request from public authorities; or
    to protect our rights, safety, or the rights and safety of others.

7. Data retention

We do not maintain user accounts or store personal profiles about you on our servers. Locally stored preferences remain on your device until you remove them by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's own policies.
8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App, including using encrypted (HTTPS) connections where available. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
9. International data transfers

Google and other service providers may process data in countries other than your own, including countries that may have different data protection laws. Where required, such transfers rely on appropriate safeguards.
10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal data, which may include the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to its processing, and to withdraw consent at any time.

    Turkey (KVKK, Law No. 6698): You may apply to the data controller under Article 11, including to learn whether your data is processed, request information about its processing, ask for correction or deletion, and object to outcomes against you.
    European Economic Area / United Kingdom (GDPR): You have the rights listed above and may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
    California (CCPA/CPRA): You have the right to know, delete, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell personal information.

Because we do not hold personal profiles about you, most requests concerning advertising data are best directed to Google or handled through your device's ad settings (Section 4). For anything else, contact us at byarchitect@disroot.org.
11. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.
